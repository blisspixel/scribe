# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Scribe's community should be respectful, constructive, and safe for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Discuss ideas and code without
personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention,
or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Respect boundaries and stop an interaction when asked.
- Keep issue, review, and discussion content relevant to the project.
- Protect credentials, recordings, transcripts, and other sensitive data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban
participants whose behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Enforcement
decisions should consider context, severity, repetition, and community safety.

To report a conduct concern, ask the maintainer for a private contact channel
without publishing sensitive details. Immediate threats should be reported to the
appropriate platform or local authorities. Vulnerabilities belong in the process
described by [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project
in another public space.
